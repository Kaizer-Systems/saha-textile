The Type Lattice focuses on exported contracts, signatures, schemas, interfaces, ports, and pricing utilities. Architecture narratives and operational guidance remain in the surrounding developer portal.

The generated surface contains the expanded catalogue contracts (`catalog`, category placement and facets, attribute definitions, product options, variants, bundles, relations, media, inventory, and content), configurable locale, currency, money, pagination and error shapes, and the revised product lifecycle. It also publishes the permission registry/grants, role and user-role-assignment contracts, `RoleRepository`, `TransactionManagerPort`, `NotificationPort`, `YouTubePort`, `VideoTranscodePort`, the auth/media/inventory/governance/extended-catalogue repositories, `UserAuthState`, `AuthUserRepository`, and atomic `nextCsrfSecretHash` rotation. The complete output contains **498 HTML pages**; persistence models and repository implementations remain absent because adapters are intentionally outside the two entry points.

Scope remains intentionally narrow: the two entry points are exactly `packages/contracts/src/index.ts` and `packages/core-domain/src/index.ts`. TypeDoc does not absorb Docusaurus narrative ownership or Scalar's HTTP-operation reference. The generated adapter uses a centred `220px / flexible / 250px` desktop grid, a 15px root scale, one page `h1`, a tablet two-column collapse, and a mobile single-column layout.
