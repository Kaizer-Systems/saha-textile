This is a source-only catalogue of the 14 Mongo models implemented today: seven original commerce/catalogue models plus seven D1 identity-support models. It measures 192 fields, 43 indexes and 13 temporary shapes; all 12 excluded-by-default hash fields are omitted from its synthetic previews. It does not promote target-only Schema Nebula collections or temporary `Mixed` fields into a finished database design. The generator never connects to local, test, staging, or production data.
