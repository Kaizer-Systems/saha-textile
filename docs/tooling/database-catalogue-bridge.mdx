This is a source-only catalogue of all **32** Mongoose models implemented today: the original seven plus identity/consent, catalogue structure, variants, bundles/relations, media, inventory, governance/notifications, FAQ, Q&A, reviews and rating aggregates. It measures **406 fields, 89 indexes and 27 temporary shapes**; all **13** excluded-by-default fields are named as policy evidence but omitted from synthetic previews. It does not promote target-only Schema Nebula collections or temporary `Mixed` fields into a finished database design. The generator never connects to local, test, staging, or production data.

Inside the catalogue, **Return to gateway** remains in the Observatory header’s right side and returns here without collapsing current-model evidence into the target Schema Nebula.
