**B3.3** PayPal receipts (net of fees, after PayPal's conversion):

**B3.4** LUT (RFD-11): who files it, and when?

**B3.5** Deadline for receiving export money, and what happens if it is late or refunded.

**B3.6** Export invoice mechanics:

- (v) Recipient: **(a) name, full address and country, no tax ID needed (Recommended)**; (b) a tax ID is needed.

**B3.7** Price shown to a customer whose delivery address is outside India (domestic prices include GST):
