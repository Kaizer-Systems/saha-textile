The source-only catalogue covers all **40** current Mongoose models: separated customer/operator populations, extracted credentials and provider identities, sessions and pending proof, authorization, consent, catalogue structure, variants, bundles and relations, media, inventory, governance and notifications, FAQ, Q&A, reviews, and rating aggregates. It measures **479 fields, 107 indexes, and 27 temporary shapes**. All **13** excluded-by-default fields are named as policy evidence but omitted from synthetic previews. Temporary `Mixed` fields are not presented as finished database design, and generation never connects to local, test, staging, or production data.
